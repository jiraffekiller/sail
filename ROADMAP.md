# Roadmap

Sail stays small: Sailing mode and heat protection, done well. Things on the list, roughly
in order. Items move here from Discussions once they are decided.

## Next
- Verify behaviour across macOS 27.x updates and keep the self-test honest about it.
- Sparkle-based in-app updates for people who do not use Homebrew.
- Optional "Discharge to lower limit now" action (powerd already supports it).
- Icon Composer (`.icon`) app icon for Liquid Glass on macOS 26+.

## Maybe
- Notifications when heat protection engages or when macOS runs its calibration.
- Localisation.

## Not planned
- Calibration cycles, schedules, statistics, MagSafe LED control.
- Intel Macs and macOS before 26.4: the mechanism Sail relies on does not exist there.
